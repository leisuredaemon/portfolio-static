ClusterGAN for Regime Discovery on Indian Stocks (NSE)

I adapted ClusterGAN from ClusterGAN paper to unlabeled NSE stock data to discover market regimes directly from high-dimensional features (OHLCV, indicators, sentiment, macro, global indices, FX/commodities). I trained G/D/E with WGAN-GP and latent consistency, swept latent std & clipping, explored cluster counts K ∈ [2, 100], and compared against K-Means.

Multi-Horizon Stock Forecasting

I created a large-scale stock market dataset covering almost all NSE-listed companies with daily data and over 80 engineered features spanning technical indicators, sentiment, macroeconomic, and global signals. Using this dataset, I built predictive models (LightGBM, early Transformer prototypes) and multi-horizon forecasts, while also simulating trades with realistic backtesting. The project provides a robust foundation for future hybrid ensemble models, portfolio optimization, and reinforcement learning–based trading strategies.

Testing with Custom Cluster based GNN Architectures

Goal : Compare multiple clustering techniques on a Benchmark dataset (Here Telco Churn dataset), understand which K (number of clusters) is appropriate for each method and understand specifically each clustering method's performance on the dataset and understand the differences and also derive insights out of it, and then propose & evaluate a custom cluster-aware GNN for the prediction and see if the custom built architecture helps in improving the performance and also if can capture the underlying patterns well and better than traditional methods like lgbm, random forest, vanilla GNNs and prove theortically that adding cluster info in the prediction improves the prediction accuracy.The goal was to make the GNNs capture the underlying patterns that can be captured with specific clustering techniques. With the insights we gathered on the performance of all the clustering techniques against the dataset and applying the same clusterng methods on the GNN we were able to derive the exact or most appropriate business factors of the nature that were resposible for the improvement of the performance which the vanilla methods or techniques failed to capture .
